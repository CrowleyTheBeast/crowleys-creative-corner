Community & The Lantern Way

Crowley's Creative Corner is meant to become more than a shelf for finished projects. The longer-term dream is a warm guild hall for creators, players, dreamers, and people finding their next step—with room for honest progress, shared quests, encouragement, and the occasional need to sit by the fire for a minute.

The Community Side of the Corner

The Lantern Way

The Lantern Way is the name for the community-building path around Crowley's Creative Corner. It is guided by a simple idea: people move forward more honestly when they do not have to pretend they have everything figured out first.

The hope is to grow a guild-hall-style space for creative conversations, personal-growth quests, videos, streams, creative challenges, collaborative projects, and the kind of encouragement that helps someone take one real next step.

This foundation is still being built. There is no public membership program or fixed community schedule yet; new pieces will be added carefully as they become real.

The Lantern Way EQ Cohort — Finding Your Next Step

This is a small-group experience for adults who feel stuck, overwhelmed, or like they are rebuilding something and could use people walking alongside them.

Eight weeks Time to practice one honest next step.
Four group meetings Every other week, about 90 minutes each.
Async check-ins Short check-ins between meetings to keep the thread going.

What We Work On

  • Name the one obstacle that keeps getting in your way.
  • Practice one honest habit for addressing it.
  • Hold each other to it without shame.

The promise is deliberately grounded: not a sweeping transformation, but one real step taken with real people. Sliding-scale rates are available at $50, $75, or $100, with no explanation required.

Important: The Lantern Way Guild is a peer-accountability and personal-growth community. It is not therapy, counseling, or crisis support, and Nikita is not a licensed therapist or counselor. If you need professional mental-health support, please contact a licensed professional or the 988 Lifeline.
