Abyss Guardians: Low Poly

Hold the twin towers above the abyss. A work-in-progress browser build, straight from the workshop.

The game is about 45 MB, so it loads when you choose to play.

On a phone or tablet the game shows touch buttons, but a keyboard and mouse play best.

Online co-op pairs you with another player. If nobody is waiting, you start a game and they join you.
